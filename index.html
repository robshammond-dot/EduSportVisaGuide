2Why this matters

What we've seen go wrong: clients who applied for their visas independently have run into two different versions of the same problem — either they didn't realise their transit country needed its own visa, or their visa dates were worked out around the event only and didn't cover the transit time either side. 3If you choose to apply yourself

You're welcome to manage your own visa application. If you do, please work through the considerations below carefully — these are the details that most often catch travellers out.

  • ✓
    Transit is part of your visa planning, not a formality Two things can catch you out: some countries require their own transit visa just to pass through, even if you never leave the airport. And separately, your visa's valid dates need to cover the whole trip — including transit time on either end — not just your time at the destination. Check both for every leg of your route.
  • ✓
    Start early — lead times are longer than you think A South African police clearance certificate alone can take 8–12 weeks to process, before the visa application itself is even lodged. Leaving this until close to departure is the most common cause of missed deadlines.
  • ✓
    Match your visa dates to your actual booking Your visa validity, entry dates and permitted duration must line up exactly with your Edusport itinerary — including any pre- or post-event travel you've added yourself.
  • ✓
    Confirm the correct visa category Tourist, event-specific and business visa categories carry different requirements. Applying under the wrong category can result in refusal or delay.
  • ✓
    Check requirements for every passport in your travelling party Family members or travel companions on different passports may face different requirements, even when travelling together on the same itinerary.
  • ✓
    Keep proof of accommodation and return travel on hand Many visa applications — and border checks — require evidence of confirmed accommodation and onward/return travel. Keep your Edusport booking confirmation accessible throughout your trip.
Worth repeating

Transit isn't a formality — check it two ways. First, does the country you're passing through require its own transit visa? Second, does your visa's validity actually cover that transit time, not just your days at the destination? Missing either one can mean denied boarding.
